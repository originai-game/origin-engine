# What Meridian Loop found

Every problem met writing this game from the knowledge package (skills and wiki) and the generated types as a game author would, in the order met. Nothing in the engine was changed; each workaround is named, and the code carries it with a comment naming the finding's number. Engine source was read only to confirm a suspected bug after it was hit (#2, #3, #13 say so). Ratings: **blocker** (the documented way does not work), **annoyance** (costs real time, or silently wrong), **nit** (small, cosmetic, docs only). Engine dfa10100 (main when the lane was taken on 2026-10-21, after wave 13's publish-testing round, so the three earlier games' findings fixed by then are not repeated); dev-profile `og` and addon built for that commit; tier metal; 2026-10-21 and 22. Commands run from the repository root, `og` = `target/debug/og`. The web part (#18 on) used `packages/engine/tests/web/dogfood.ts` and `published.ts` from main 85174856 against the same engine build (see the end).

Top five: #3, #12, #9, #8, #2 (with #6).

1. **og-feel §12 has no vehicle row, and og.Vehicle's defaults drive a chassis of a few kilograms** — annoyance (docs). og-game §13 gives og.Vehicle one line ("a raycast car (3D, `throttle`, `steer`, `brake`; wheels from `og.physics.vehicleWheels`)") and og-feel §12, "Controllers by genre", tunes the platformer and the character controller only. The field docs give units but not the scale: `engineForce` 80 is newtons per driven wheel and `brakeForce` 30 an absolute force, while the suspension is scaled by the chassis's mass (rapier's raycast vehicle, after Bullet's), so the defaults suit the mass a box chassis gets at `density` 1 (1.8 × 0.6 × 4 m: 4.3 kg). Given a car's 1,000 kg (`og.RigidBody.mass`), full throttle accelerates it at 0.16 m/s²: 0.53 m/s after 3 s. Nothing says which numbers go together for a car of real mass, and at that mass `maxSpeed` 20 was overshot (23.6 m/s after 5 s of full throttle on flat ground, though the force "fades out towards it"). Repro: `og run examples/meridian-loop --headless --commands "0.5:race:go;0.6:set:Tuning.engineForce=80;1:key_down:KeyW;4:get:Race.kmh;4.1:quit"` (the Chassis system writes `Tuning.engineForce` to the vehicle every tick). Workaround: tuned by measurement in a fixture, then the game's `Tuning`: 1,000 kg, `engineForce` 5,200, `brakeForce` 2,600, `maxSpeed` 46, `grip` 0.62, `maxSteer` 0.5 (`src/components.ts`, `src/world.ts`).
2. **A raycast car rolls over at moderate lock and nothing in og.Vehicle or og.RigidBody stops it; there is no force, torque or impulse call** — annoyance (API gap). With a box chassis (1.8 × 0.4 × 4 m, 1,000 kg, the wheels mounted at its centre) a 0.4 lock at 30 m/s puts the car on its roof within two seconds (`og.Transform.quaternion` [0.82, 0.02, 0.55, -0.13]); the lateral grip is near unlimited below the friction slip, and the centre of mass sits where the collider is. There is no centre-of-mass field on og.RigidBody, no roll influence or side-friction stiffness on og.Vehicle (`crates/og-physics/src/vehicle3.rs` fixes `side_friction_stiffness` at 1 and rapier keeps `roll_influence` at 0.1), and og.physics has no `applyForce`/`applyImpulse`/`applyTorque` (og.Velocity's doc: "Set it to launch or stop a body"), so an anti-roll bar or downforce, the usual arcade fixes, have no call. Repro: a fixture of the og.Vehicle doc's fields (a 1,000 kg box chassis with `mountHeight` 0, `suspensionRest` 0.2, `wheelRadius` 0.35, `engineForce` 5,000, `grip` 2), throttle 1 for 5 s, then steer 0.4; read the quaternion at 9 s. Workaround: a flat chassis box (0.25 m) with the wheel mounts 0.3 m above its centre (`mountHeight` -0.3), so the centre of mass rides low between the wheels, and the assists written as small changes of og.Velocity every tick: the roll and pitch rates damped by 12 %, a downforce of 0.004 v² with four wheels down (`src/car.ts` Chassis).
3. **BLOCKER: og.Vehicle's wheel rays land on any collider below them, whatever its og.Collider `layers` and `mask`** — so two cars that must not touch (a ghost replaying the best lap's inputs beside the player, the documented way to keep bodies apart being the layers: "Two colliders interact when each one's layers meet the other's mask") stand on each other. A ghost car starts every lap on the player's own spot, so the input-replay ghost the brief asked for cannot be built. Confirmed after hitting it: `vehicle3.rs` builds its ray filter as `QueryFilter::new().exclude_rigid_body(chassis).exclude_sensors()`, with no collision groups. Repro, in the game: `og run examples/meridian-loop --headless --commands "0.5:race:go;1:twin;3:get:car.og.Transform.position;3.1:quit"` (`twin` spawns a second og.Vehicle on the car's spot on layer 4, meeting only the circuit, the car's mask leaving it out): the car stands at y 0.58 on the other's chassis instead of 0.35 on the road. In a 15-line fixture (two such cars on a ground box, `brake` 1, nothing else) both climb: y 3.0 after one second, 5.6 after two. Workaround: the ghost is not a car: the best lap's poses are taped (a pose every second tick, 15 characters) and the ghost model is drawn from them (`src/ghost.ts`, `src/tape.ts`); the engine's determinism is still exercised, by the replay (#12).
4. **A component has no array field, and a `str` field holds at most 4,096 bytes, which no skill or wiki page says** — annoyance. A lap's inputs or poses (a ghost's tape) are a few thousand values; og-core §4 and `reference/reflect.md` list the column kinds (no array) and describe `str(capacity)` without a limit, so a 40,500-byte `str` typechecks and is refused only when the game runs: `the bundle has refused declarations: 'Tape.cur': a string field holds 1 to 4096 bytes, got a capacity of 40500`. Repro: `CHUNK` 40500 in `src/components.ts`, then `og run examples/meridian-loop --headless --commands "1:quit"`. Workaround: each tape cut into 3,990-byte `str` chunks, one entity each (28 entities: `src/tapes.ts`), written through the column's bytes in the system that owns them.
5. **An asset handle does not fit a `u32` field, and nothing says which column holds one** — nit. A texture handle kept in a resource (`minimap: u32(0)`) refuses `start`: `GameDesc.start threw, so the world is half-built and does not run: Error: Race.minimap: 4294967298 is not a u32`. og-game §17 says "Asset handles are saved as numbers"; the column table has `f64` but does not say a handle needs it. Repro: `minimap: u32(0)` in `src/components.ts`. Workaround: `f64` (what Kestrel Stair's `music` field also uses, found there).
6. **A system's query is every component of its reads and writes together, and `world.query` is refused inside a system; neither is said** — annoyance (docs). og-core §4 and `SystemDesc` say components "also shape its query", not that an entity must have all of them; a system that reads one named entity's component (the car's `Racer`) and loops over another archetype (the skid marks' `Mark` and `og.Decal`) gets no archetypes at all, silently. Reaching for `world.query({ with: [...] })` inside the system answers `a system receives its query as its first argument; world.query is for commands and callbacks`. Repro: add `Racer` to the Marks system's writes in `src/fx.ts`: no skid mark is laid (`get:mark-0.og.Decal.opacity` stays 0 through a slide). Workaround: one system per loop, with only the looped archetype's components declared (Marks apart from Fx, Tapes apart from Race and Driver, the Voices system reading the throttle from the Race resource instead of the car's `Racer`); what crosses between them goes through the Race resource.
7. **The neon-vector preset's outline washes a large ground plane: unbounded near the camera with `outlineDistance`, a pale band along the horizon without it** — annoyance. `MaterialStyle.outlineDistance`: "nearer they grow, farther they thin in proportion", with no cap; with the preset's outline (`outlineColor` #40f0ff) at 1.4 px and `outlineDistance` 120 (to keep the far barriers' lines), the road a metre under a chase camera is outlined about 170 px wide and the frame from the horizon down is one pale cyan wash, road, ground and car under it. At a constant width (`outlineDistance` 0, 1.5 px) the near frame is right, but the ground (one 1.6 km mesh seen at a grazing angle) still draws a pale cyan band some 30 px tall along the horizon, hiding the grid's far lines and the skyline's foot. og-taste §1 names neon-vector for racing, which is all ground plane, and says nothing of either. Repro: `og run examples/meridian-loop --headless --commands "0.5:race:go;0.6:drive:auto;0.7:set:og.MaterialStyle.outlineWidth=1.4;0.7:set:og.MaterialStyle.outlineDistance=120;6.3:screenshot:flood.png;6.4:quit"` (the flood); `og run examples/meridian-loop --headless --commands "1.2:set:og.MaterialStyle.outlineWidth=1.5;1.4:screenshot:band.png;1.5:quit"` (the band at the title). Workaround: `outlineWidth 0` in `looks/meridian.ogt`; the neon is the circuit's emissive tubes and bloom.
8. **og.ui.racingHud's own words are English and fixed, and it has no place for a time trial's numbers** — annoyance. In German the HUD still says `lap` under the lap, `Best` before the record, `Pause` on its button (its accessible name too) and `Boost` on a meter: `RacingHudDesc` has a field for the speed's `unit` and none for these words, unlike the templates fixed in wave 10 (og-i18n §2 lists every other template's words as desc fields). It always draws a place (`1/1` in a time trial: `position` and `racers` cannot be hidden) and the boost meter (0 still draws it), and has no field for splits, a delta, a sector or the last lap, which every time trial shows: `time` is one number. Repro: `tests/locales.og.ts` asserts the four English words in German (`og test examples/meridian-loop`). Workaround: the splits, the delta and the countdown are the game's own capsules beside the template (`src/ui.ts` `sectors`); the English words and the unused place and meter stay.
9. **og.ui.racingHud takes Back even under an open dialog: Escape and the pad's B never close pause or the settings over it** — annoyance (bug). og-ui §4: "Escape and B go back: the topmost dialog or menu closes". With the racing HUD drawn under `og.ui.pauseMenu` (as Kestrel Stair draws its HUD under pause), Escape answers nothing: the pause menu stays (Enter on Resume still works), and the same over the settings, so a keyboard or pad player is stuck in pause unless they find Resume. Without the HUD that tick, Escape closes both. Repro: in `src/ui.ts` call `hud(world, r, p)` while paused (the line commented "FINDINGS #9"), then `og run examples/meridian-loop --headless --commands "1.6:race:go;2:open:pause;2.5:key_down:Escape;2.534:key_up:Escape;3:get:Race.paused;3.1:quit"` (true). Workaround: the HUD is not drawn while pause or the settings are open.
10. **og.ui.resultsScreen's score is a whole number counted up, with no label: a time trial's result cannot be its headline** — annoyance. `ResultsDesc.score` and `best` are numbers, counted up and shown as integers (1:36.233 passed as 96.233 s showed "96"); there is no field for a time, a format, a unit or "lower is better", and `bestLabel` fills `{best}` with that number. Repro: pass `score: r.total / 60` in `src/ui.ts` `results`. Workaround: the score is points (100,000 over the race's seconds: 1,039 for 1:36.233), the times are stats rows, and `bestLabel` is given the lap record already formatted.
11. **world.snapshot() carries the level's triangle-mesh colliders: a 956 m circuit's world is 400 KB of the slot's 512 KiB** — annoyance. `og.physics.setMesh`'s doc says "meshes are assets, not world state", but the snapshot grows with the collider geometry: in a fixture an empty world is 10,834 bytes, 28 entities of 3,990-byte `str` chunks 11,834, the circuit's two models instantiated 11,890, and the road's and walls' triangle-mesh colliders (about 6,700 triangles) 395,290. The game's replay start is 400,998 bytes, seven parts of `saveSlot`'s eight; og-game §17's sizes (11 KiB to 78 KB) say nothing of colliders. Repro: `og run examples/meridian-loop --headless --commands "0.5:race;2:get:Race.snapBytes;2.1:quit"` (400,998). Workaround: none needed yet (it fits); a longer circuit would not.
12. **A world.snapshot() taken in a system and restored skips the rest of that tick (the systems after it and the physics step), which no page says; a replay from it diverges** — annoyance (docs). `world.snapshot` is "the world as that system sees it" and `restore` "applies when the tick ends"; a replay restored from a snapshot taken in the Race system (the fifth) ran a different race (lap 1 in 2,958 ticks against 2,046: the restored world was one physics step behind its countdown, and the run's inputs then met a different car). Taken in the first system of the tick, the restored world runs that whole tick again and the replay matches to the tick (2,046, 1,859, 1,841 against 2,046, 1,859, 1,841). Repro: call `saveStart` from the Race system after the countdown's decrement (src/race.ts) instead of from Ui, then `og test examples/meridian-loop` (`rerun.og.ts` fails). Workaround: the snapshot taken by the first system of a tick (`src/ui.ts`), noted in `src/replay.ts`.
13. **The missing-glyph warning always says tick 0 and fires once a process** — nit. The minus sign of a split's delta (U+2212, outside the neon pack's `charset latin punct`), drawn first at tick 2,603 (the second lap's first split), was reported `WARN tick 0`, and had the title's arrows (↑ ↓ ← →, also missing) still been there, it would never have been named: `crates/og-runtime/src/engine.rs` logs render notes with `"tick": 0` and warns once (`missing_warned`). The warning's advice (`font { chars }`) was right. Repro: `−` back in `deltaText` (`src/race.ts`), `og run examples/meridian-loop --headless --commands "0.5:race:go;0.6:drive:auto;50:quit"` (the second lap's first split, the first delta, is at 47 s). Workaround: ASCII arrows' words and `-`.
14. **og.Model has no `visible`** — nit (API gap). og.Mesh, og.ParticleSystem and og.Decal can be hidden; a glTF instance cannot, so the ghost between laps is moved under the world (y -50) with its wheels. Repro: `grep -n "visible" packages/knowledge/wiki/load.md` (none under og.Model). Workaround: `hideGhost` in `src/ghost.ts`.
15. **No texture repeat: a plane geometry has no UV scale and StandardMaterialDesc no `repeat`** — nit. A ground grid repeating every 10 m (three.js `texture.repeat`) cannot be made with `og.render.planeGeometry` and `standardMaterial`. Workaround: the ground is a glTF of its own whose UVs run past 1 with a repeating sampler (`tools/circuit.ts` `groundModel`).
16. **WheelPose has no slip, and og.Vehicle reads back only `speed` and `contacts`** — nit (API gap). Tyre smoke, skid marks and the squeal want each wheel's slide, which rapier's controller knows (its skid info); the game derives the rear axle's sideways speed from og.Velocity instead (`src/car.ts`), which misses wheelspin.
17. **Nothing in the knowledge package says how a game records and replays its own play, or makes a ghost** — nit (docs). "Replay" is used throughout (og-core §2: answers are host input "a replay records them like a key press"; og-build §4; og-feel §13) for the probe's and playtests' re-runs; a game author asking for a ghost or a Watch-replay feature finds no recorder, no pattern and no word on which way works: re-driving a second body from recorded inputs (#3 blocks it for cars), recording poses (`src/ghost.ts`), or restoring a snapshot and feeding inputs (#11, #12 meet it). Workaround: the game tapes its intents and poses itself (`src/tape.ts`, `src/tapes.ts`).

## What worked well

The engine's determinism carries a racing game whole. Watch replay restores
a 400 KB world through `og.platform.loadSlot` and `world.restore` and the
taped inputs drive the same three laps to the tick (5,746 ticks, every lap
and sector equal); a session of slides, the jump, resets, pause and the
ghost ends on one world hash in two processes; the autopilot's laps are the
same ticks in every run, so a playtest can name the tick a lap ends on.
og.Vehicle, once given a car's numbers, drove the whole circuit: banked
bends, a 1.2 s flight over the gap and its landing, slides into the walls,
the wheel models from `og.physics.vehicleWheels` turning and steering on
the road. A glTF written by a script cooked with its
`KHR_materials_clearcoat` and `KHR_materials_emissive_strength` (the car's
lacquer, the neon tubes) and its repeating sampler, and two triangle-mesh
colliders made a 956 m circuit with no seam a car could catch on. og cook:
41 assets cold in 4.4 s, about 70 ms after a change, no warnings. Every
template asked for (main menu, pause, settings with every word from the
tables, results, leaderboard with its honest states) worked from its desc
on the neon pack, in German too (umlauts in the pack's Latin-1 faces, no
other step); og.platform's store, settings, leaderboard and a seven-part
`saveSlot` worked the first time, across a `store: "keep"` relaunch too.
A looping engine voice re-pitched every tick, a squeal faded by the slide,
the countdown on one-shot voices and the chunked music made a mix
`listen` heard unclipped. Skid marks as a ring of `og.Decal`s, smoke by an
emitter's start and stop ticks, sparks at `og.CollisionStarted`'s point and
the camera's shake, kick and punch needed no more than their docs. Every
refusal named its fix: the undeclared `Racer`, the 4,096-byte `str`, the
`u32` handle, `world.query` in a system, a font's missing glyphs.
