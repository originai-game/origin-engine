# What Brasswick found

Every problem met writing this game from the knowledge package (skills and wiki) and the generated types as a game author would, in the order met. Nothing in the engine was changed; each workaround is named, and the code carries it with a comment naming the finding's number. Engine source was not read: `packages/engine/src/playtest.ts` was read for the playtest options, and `examples/hollowbell` and the runner archetype (which the skills point to) for patterns. Ratings: **blocker** (the documented way does not work), **annoyance** (costs real time, or silently wrong), **nit** (small, cosmetic, docs only). Engine 21b0b0cc (main after wave 13, so Lantern Vale's, Hollowbell's, The Kestrel Stair's and the web round's findings fixed there are not repeated; Meridian Loop's, on its own lane, were read and are not repeated either, #9 says where one meets it), 2026-10-22; dev-profile `og` (`target/debug/og`) and addon; tier metal; the web play on WebGPU with the lane's publish modules (built 2026-10-21 18:07, the newest there). Main 9fe3a417 (wave 13's racing-ui, vehicle-physics, ui-five and templates-title rounds) was merged at the end: its docs and IDL were read again against every finding, and only #9 is fixed there (below); the lane's `og` and addon predate the merge and there is no `cargo` here, so nothing was re-run on it. Commands run from the repository root, `og` = `target/debug/og`.

Top five: #6, #13, #9, #10, #15 (with #4).

1. **`create` has no 2D game: a 2D premise gets the 3D template** — annoyance. og-game "Writing a game" step 1 says the scaffold writes `project.ogt`; `bun packages/create/bin/create.ts <dir> --premise "a short 2D puzzle platformer in a lamplit clockwork vault: a little brass automaton pushes crates onto pressure plates; warm, wondrous, quiet; modern pixel art" --no-prompt` writes `profile "3d"`, three glTF models, a navmesh, a guard's behaviour, `scenes/main.ogt` and the `main.ts` of a 3D yard whatever the premise says; there is no `--profile 2d` (`create --help`), and the premise picked `monochrome-pixel` (a one-ink look) for a warm brass vault. Repro: that command, then `grep profile <dir>/project.ogt; ls <dir>/assets`. Workaround: `--look detailed-pixel`, then `assets/{ai,nav,models,textures,scenes,audio,fonts}`, the two template playtests and the template's `main.ts` deleted and `profile "2d"` set (and `scenes`/`scene` dropped) by hand; nothing says which of the template's files a 2D game keeps. **Fixed on `platformer-2d`** (this stream's last commit): `create --profile 2d` writes the 2D starter `templates/game-2d` (a courier's platformer: sprites and flipbooks, a tilemap with one-way ledges, 2D physics, a HUD and pause menu, one playtest), and so does a premise that says 2D, platformer, side-scroller, metroidvania or pixel art without saying 3D; the published package carries it as `template-2d/`, and the install test scaffolds and tests it. The look is still the catalogue's match for the premise, or `--look`.
2. **The flipbook grammar is not in the knowledge package** — annoyance (docs). og-game §8 gives every line of a tileset, a tilemap and an emitter, and of a flipbook only a sentence ("grid or named frames, or an Aseprite / TexturePacker JSON `atlas`; clips with `fps`, `mode loop | pingPong | once` and `event <frame> "name"`"): `texture`, `grid <w> <h> <cols> <rows>`, `spacing`, `clip <name> { frames ... }`, `durations` and `pivot` (og-feel §9 names `pivot 0.5 0`, not where it goes) have no line, and nothing says the clips are indexed in file order (what `og.anim.clip` answers, so what a system comparing `og.Flipbook.clip` with a constant relies on). Repro: `grep -rn "clip idle\|spacing\|pivot" packages/knowledge/skills`. Workaround: the shape of `templates/archetypes/runner/assets/sprites/courier.ogt` (og-game §8 names the runner); `start` in `src/main.ts` checks Wick's clips are in file order (#2), because `src/hero.ts` keeps the clip as an index. **Fixed on `platformer-2d`** by 54937d3d (og-game §8 gives the flipbook grammar line by line, clip order included).
3. **A one-shot emitter spawned ahead of its first use fires at tick 0** — nit. og-game §10 restarts a one-shot effect "by writing the tick to its `og.ParticleSystem.startTick`"; spawned in `start` with `startTick` 0 (the default) each burst plays once at the spawn point on the first tick, and a start in the past is refused (`'og.ParticleSystem.startTick': -1000 is not a u32`, so `GameDesc.start` throws). Repro: `startTick: -1000` for the effects' emitters in `src/level.ts`, then `og run examples/brasswick --headless --commands "0.5:quit"`. Workaround: `visible: false` until the Effects system first moves and restarts one (#3 in `src/level.ts`, `src/feel.ts`). **Fixed on `platformer-2d`** by 8dc42e6a (`PARTICLES_ARMED`, `startTick`'s default: a one-shot never given a tick waits armed).
4. **og.ui.platformerHud draws hearts and a score a game does not have** — annoyance. With `lives: 0, maxLives: 0` the template still draws an empty hearts capsule (`Lives`, 24 × 32) top-left and a `Score` capsule reading `0` top-right whatever is passed, and the level's name is a bare 13 px label under the empty capsule, without a frame, over the play field; `PlatformerHudDesc` has no field to leave either out. A puzzle-platformer (gears, no lives, no score) cannot use it. Repro: `og.ui.platformerHud("Hud", { lives: 0, maxLives: 0, coins: 2, level: "The Boiler Stair", timeLeft: null })` in place of `hud` in `src/ui.ts`, then `og run examples/brasswick --headless --commands "0.5:play:1;1.6:dump_ui:hud.json;1.7:quit"` (`Lives` row with no child, `Points` label `0`). Workaround: the HUD from widgets, capsules with an icon and a number as og-taste §2 asks, and a d-pad and Jump when `og.ui.frameViewport().coarsePointer` (#4 in `src/ui.ts` `hud`).
5. **A map's collision layers all take one og.Tilemap.colliderLayers** — nit (API gap). og-game §8's grammar marks a layer `collision true`, and og.PlatformerController's one-way platforms are colliders on `oneWayLayers`; a layer of grates and the plate terrain of one map both become colliders on `og.Tilemap.colliderLayers` (no per-layer bits in the grammar or in `TileLayerInfo`), so a one-way tile layer cannot be told apart. Hollowbell met the same and did the same. Repro: `collision true` on the `planks` layer in `tools/make-assets.ts`, then a jump from under a grate meets its underside. Workaround: the grates are drawn by the `planks` layer and collided by `oneway` objects, a thin static box each on `Bits.Grate` (#5 in `src/level.ts`). **Fixed on `platformer-2d`** by 139f294b (a map's collision layer names its own `colliderLayers` and `colliderMask`, as a line or a Tiled property).
6. **og.PlatformerController shoves any dynamic body at its full run speed, whatever its mass** — annoyance. Its doc does not say it pushes (og.CharacterController's does, "pushes dynamic bodies it walks into", and has `pushBodies` to turn it off); it does, and a 1,000 kg crate moves at the controller's 6.4 m/s like a 2 kg one, so a crate puzzle has no weight and nothing dynamic can be made too heavy to push. Repro: `og run examples/brasswick --headless --commands "0.3:set:Tuning.pushSpeed=6.5;0.5:play:1;0.6:warp:26.5:-16;0.7:set:l1-crate-0.og.RigidBody.mass=1000;1.0:key_down:ArrowRight;1.3:get:l1-crate-0.og.Transform.position;1.6:get:l1-crate-0.og.Transform.position;1.7:quit"` (x 28.96, then 30.87). Workaround: while a crate is just ahead (`og.physics.overlap`, mask `Bits.Crate`) Wick's `maxSpeed` drops to `Tuning.pushSpeed` 2.4 (#6 in `src/hero.ts`); a heavy or fixed body is not possible. **Fixed on `platformer-2d`** by 8f226ceb (`pushBodies` and `pushStrength` on both controllers: the push is an impulse capped by the strength, so a crate's mass tells). The `Tuning.pushSpeed` slowdown stays, now as the game's push speed rather than a workaround: the capped push is a force, which takes the 2 kg crates up to Wick's run speed and past their plates (debt round 2, `physics-push`: without it the first level's crate stops at x 34.75 against the plate's 31.5 and the gate's plate is never held); heavier crates (density 40 to 160) either overshoot or hold, and damped ones fall like feathers off the second level's slab (`tests/evidence/debt2/physics-push/NOTES.md`).
7. **og.platform's slot answers carry no time** — nit. og-platform-sdk §1: "Every answer carries `time` (UTC ms)"; under `og run` the `store.get` answers do (1792584842280, the host's clock) while `slot.load` and `slot.save` answer `time` 0. Repro: `og run examples/brasswick --headless --commands "0.5:dump_platform:calls.json;0.6:quit"` (the third call, `slot.load` with `metaOnly`, beside the first two). Workaround: none needed (the game reads no time). **Fixed on `platformer-2d`** by db23bde2 (a slot call's answer carries the time of the store answer that ended it).
8. **Nothing marks the tick after a restore, and every place in the world rolls back** — annoyance. og-game §17: "keep request numbers and the session's own state (a best score read from the store, an open menu) where a restore cannot roll them back: reset them after it"; but every resource and component rolls back (og-core §2 forbids module variables), and there is no event, callback or flag for "a restore applied at the end of the last tick" (`grep -rn -i "restored\|onRestore" packages/engine/types/index.d.ts` finds only the probe's). A restored world comes back with the request numbers of the moment it was saved (answered long ago) and that moment's settings resource, while the language, the volumes and the store live on in the session. Workaround: `Game.restored` is set true just around `world.snapshot()`, so only saved worlds hold it; the Answers system sees it on the tick after a restore, clears every request number, asks for the settings, the progress and the slot again, and toasts (#8 in `src/save.ts`); `tests/save.og.ts` checks the settings and the toast after Continue. **Fixed on `platformer-2d`** by 0bb840b1 (`og.WorldRestored`, delivered the tick after a restore with the tick it came from and the saved one).
9. **og.ui.back() asked before the tick's dialogs are built takes their Back: pause and the settings never close on Escape** — annoyance. og-ui §4: "Escape and B go back: the topmost dialog or menu closes, otherwise `og.ui.back()` answers true once (open pause on it)", with no word on order; a Ui system that asks `og.ui.back()` first (to open pause) and then builds `pauseMenu` and `settingsScreen` gets true while they are open, and neither closes (pauseMenu's "Back answers `resume`" never comes). Likely Meridian Loop's #9 (the racing HUD taking Back under an open dialog) seen from the game's side. Repro: move the pause block of the Ui system in `src/ui.ts` above `og.ui.screenTransition`, then `og run examples/brasswick --headless --commands "0.5:play:1;1.0:open_pause;1.6:key_down:Escape;1.65:key_up:Escape;1.9:get:Game.paused;1.95:quit"` (true). Workaround: `og.ui.back()` is asked after the screens and the settings are built, and only while no dialog of the game's is open (#9 in `src/ui.ts`). **Fixed on main** by 97df3400 (meridian-loop #9: `og.ui.back()` answers false while a dialog Back closes is open, asked before it or not; og-ui §4 says so), seen in the merge, not re-run (the lane's addon predates it); the workaround stays harmless.
10. **og.ui.levelSelect has no way out for a pointer or a finger** — annoyance. A mouse or phone player who opens Levels must pick a level. Repro: `og run examples/brasswick --headless --commands "0.5:levels;1.0:touch_move:100,100;1.05:touch_start;1.1:touch_end;1.5:get:Game.levelsOpen;1.6:dump_ui:lv.json;1.7:quit"` (still open; no button under `Levels/Panel`). Workaround: none in the game (#10 in `src/ui.ts`). **Fixed on main** by [80008fbb](https://github.com/originai-game/origin-engine/commit/80008fbba178233273c6cf455bf398bdc1ead5ef) (the level select has a Back button in its footer and a tap on its scrim answers `closed`, as Escape and B do; `packages/engine/tests/ui-levels.test.ts`), confirmed in r4 author-loop (`tests/evidence/r4/author-loop/NOTES.md`).

13. **og run ends at the first tick past the script's last step when a restore moves og.Time forward: the steps after it never run** — annoyance (bug). world.restore's doc: the command channel's scheduled steps "keep their remaining delays though og.Time takes the saved tick"; but a Continue at 2.45 s restoring a world saved at tick 214 ended the run at tick 215 (`og run --json`: `"tick": 215`, `"stepped": 145`), with no `quit`, no warning and none of the later steps run, so the playtest failed on files the run never wrote. A Continue after the saved tick (og.Time moved back) runs the rest of the script as documented. Repro: `og test examples/brasswick` (it leaves the save test's store at `examples/brasswick/og-out/test/save/.store/platform.json`); copy it, then `og run examples/brasswick --headless --platform-store <copy> --commands "2.4:touch_move_to:continue;2.417:touch_start;2.45:touch_end;2.6:get:Game.phase;3.3:get:Game.phase;3.4:quit" --json` (no `get` logged). Workaround: the relaunch taps Continue at 4.0 s, after the saved tick (#13 in `tests/save.og.ts`). **Fixed on `platformer-2d`** by f7acf2e8 (the script ends when its last step has run, wherever a restore moved og.Time).

Workaround: the whole HUD in the top-right corner (#15 in `src/ui.ts` `hud`). **Fixed on main** by [41f49c78](https://github.com/originai-game/origin-engine/commit/41f49c7853a3e22b425ae66b401e099591cebff2) (anchored groups keep out of `Viewport.covered`, the shell's Back) and [80008fbb](https://github.com/originai-game/origin-engine/commit/80008fbba178233273c6cf455bf398bdc1ead5ef) (a corner group steps down past it where moving along the edge would land on another group; og-ui `a_corner_group_steps_down_past_the_control_when_moving_along_lands_on_another`, `packages/engine/tests/ui-hud-corner.test.ts`); og-ui §5 says to build a game's own HUD from `og.ui.anchor` groups, as Brasswick's is. 16. **An answer applied by a system after the one that builds the title shows the defaults behind the play gate** — nit (docs). og-platform-sdk §3 says to apply an answer the tick it arrives, so the title behind the gate shows the saved language; it does not say the system that reads answers must run before the one that builds the screens. With the settings read in a system after `Ui`, the frame the shell primes is that tick's English title: the web play's reload had `Partida nueva` missing behind the gate, though the answer had landed that tick. Workaround: an `Answers` system first in `GameDesc.systems` reads every answer, `Saves` later writes (#16 in `src/save.ts`); the second web play read the title in Spanish behind the gate. **Fixed on main** by [80008fbb](https://github.com/originai-game/origin-engine/commit/80008fbba178233273c6cf455bf398bdc1ead5ef) (og-platform-sdk §3: the system that reads the answers comes before the one that builds the title, `systems: [Answers, ..., Ui, Saves]`), confirmed in r4 author-loop.

## What worked well

og cook: 54 assets cold in 2.8 s, about 50 ms after a change, no warnings, and the engine's own `type tilemap` grammar for three maps written by a script (a blob47 terrain with collision, numeric layers, `parallax`, `repeat x`, `lit false`, objects with props) cooked the first time; its refusal of a `pause` command ("'pause' is a core action; register under another name") named the fix. og.PlatformerController gave a run, a full jump to the height asked at its time to apex, coyote time, a jump buffer, wall slides held under `wallSlideSpeed`, wall jumps up a three-cell shaft, grates dropped through, a lift ridden and crates shoved, with no stutter or sink on tile seams. Dynamic crates tipped off a slab onto a plate and dropped into a pit; a kinematic door moved by its og.Velocity and a gate on a prismatic og.Joint with a position motor slid exactly their travel; `og.physics.overlap` made the plates; og.Lighting2D lit the map's layers with lamps, wall lamps and Wick's flame, crates casting shadows and the far layer left out by `lit false`. world.snapshot() of a 115-entity 2D world (22,574 bytes) went through saveSlot and came back through loadSlot and world.restore across a relaunch with the crates, the lamp, the gear taken and the tick stomped; the first level played by keys alone (gears, the pit, two crates, a plate and its door, four wall jumps, the hatch) ended on one hash in two processes. The title, level select, pause, settings and results templates took every word from the tables (Spanish accents and `¿¡ñ` from the fantasy pack's Latin-1 faces, nothing else to do). The published build played on WebGPU in Chrome: first frame and title at 290 ms, lanes on (8 Workers), 60 ticks a second with step 1.5 ms and render 0.6 ms (p50) over 600 ticks, a clean console, the settings saved and read back after a reload (in Spanish behind the gate after #16's fix), Back reporting `end`.
