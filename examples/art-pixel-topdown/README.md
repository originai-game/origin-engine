# Art sample: pixel-topdown

The pixel-topdown family's sample scene of the art wave (docs/art.md §2.6): a hero, a
stepped tree, a crate and a lantern standing on a tile map
(`assets/maps/meadow.ogt`: grass, a raised ledge with its cliff face and
steps, an auto-tiled path and pond), made through `artBrief` and conformed
by the cook to the look `project.ogt` names. The tile set
(`assets/art/terrain.png` and `.ogt`: 16 plain tiles and three blob47
terrains) is made by `tools/art-samples/tiles.py` from the generated sheet
`sources/terrain-sheet.png`; the cook conforms it per tile.
How it is made and run under each style of the family:
`tools/art-samples/README.md`; the frames and verdicts:
`tests/evidence/art/samples/NOTES.md`.
