# Release 0.7.1 (2026-10-28 UTC)

All five npm packages are published at 0.7.1, and `latest` points to 0.7.1.
Tag `v0.7.1` is pushed at `57263610d515dde754e16e5a96f03139e379a479`, the
commit `release.yml` run 40685163948 checked out.

This is a patch release (docs/release.md §3) with the simulation
**unchanged** and no IDL member changed. `changelog.json` records no
breaking or added entries and four fixed lines:

- F1 (`b281b8fc`): the cook's mesh conformance drops faces thinner than the
  position grid and keeps flat normals on lathe poles and on voxelised
  parts turned by their placement. Conformed models re-cook (model desc
  `conform-mesh-v3`).
- `RenderScene::set_shader_param` writes a changed parameter into the
  material's uniform in place and keeps its bind group (`9ccaf706`).
  Frames are unchanged.
- F2 (`aa49ce7c`): `bayer4` and `bayer8` (`Stylise.dither`,
  `ArtPalette.dither`) use the dispersed Bayer matrix instead of a
  quadrant-clustered one. Every frame and cook-conformed texture dithered
  with them changes; `bayer2` does not.
- The rubber-hose-animation and gothic-contour presets drop posterize,
  which had turned their sepia and blue-grey art into olive, pink and
  violet bands.
