# Release 0.6.0 (2026-10-26 UTC)

All five npm packages are published at 0.6.0. Tag `v0.6.0` is pushed at
`34b492d0b96e7139c6ed288d48627ed04c3f3b66`, the source of every selected
tarball. Registry SHA-512 integrity matches all five tarballs. The release
receipt was integrated on top of `c0f836fb`; its later Actions workflow
changes were not part of the published packages.

## Rejected candidate and accepted differences

The initial `5ec3143d` candidate was not published. Its functional lane
passed, but Rumpus changed by RMSE 0.00050–0.00439 despite zero outliers.
Inspection localized the changes to island rims, undersides and shadows,
not UI text or open sea. The coordinator traced this to screen AO being
applied to water unconditionally. The release source gates that behavior
on PostEffects.aoDirect, restoring the default. The final Rumpus results
above were measured again on Metal after that fix.

Water-3d still reports RMSE 0.00062, max_diff 132, four outliers (0.0004%)
on Metal. These are x468–471, y375: a gray patch becomes cyan in open
water, away from the shoreline and bright sun-glint region. The coordinator
accepted this as a pre-existing reflection discontinuity: its lavapipe
comparison at pre-fork `ae0e6281` and release `34b492d0` was identical in
metrics, RMSE 0.00167, max_diff 132, eight outliers (0.0009%) against Metal.
That cross-revision evidence is the coordinator's measurement, not a Mac
rerun of the old commit. This scene has no AO; the AO fix does not affect it.

The coordinator also accepted Metal-only UI field-border rounding:
text-fields-2 RMSE 0.00136 and text-mixed-runs 0.00055, both max RGB
difference 3, zero outliers, and zero AE at 1% fuzz. Difference masks show
field borders, not glyphs. The max differences were computed from the
PNGs; the UI harness does not print them. The coordinator reported eleven
lavapipe UI frames byte-identical across the old and new revisions.
No references or tolerances were changed during this release run.

## Publication and registry verification

then engine and create-game, with the injected NPM_TOKEN kept out of logs.
`git push origin v0.6.0` succeeded.

dist.integrity check matched SHA-512 of every selected tarball.

exit 0, DX12 doctor frame 1072 ms. Two npm preflights returned ETARGET;
