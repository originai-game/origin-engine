# Release 0.7.2 (2026-10-29 UTC)

All five npm packages are published at 0.7.2, and `latest` points to 0.7.2.
Tag `v0.7.2` is pushed at `ccda9a97355c238dd889a875fbf72a29d31092d4`, the
commit `release.yml` run 40822787700 checked out.

The simulation is **unchanged** and there are no breaking entries.
`changelog.json` records the release as `kind: patch`, because
`scripts/release.sh bump` derives the kind from the version numbers and
0.7.1 → 0.7.2 is a patch. It adds IDL members
(`MaterialStyle.shadowDarkness`, `surface`, `surfaceStrength`,
`surfaceScale` and the `MaterialSurface` enum), but they are additive and
off by default. The coordinator had called it a minor; the recorded kind
is patch.

## Changelog entries

Added:

- `MaterialStyle.shadowDarkness` (0 to 1, 0 off): where the sun does not
  reach, a lit surface loses that share of the sun and of the ambient light.
  `RenderCapabilities.stylise` names it.
- The cook paints a model's own colour textures with the look's Stylise
  paint fields in texel space when the project's look has an art contract
  that conforms models. Painted 3D textures re-cook.
- `conform character`: the cook holds such a model to
  `ArtShape.characterFaces`; a type model past its face budget is rebuilt
  with fewer segments before it is decimated.
- `MaterialStyle.surface` (`MaterialSurface` none, clay, paper, plastic),
  `surfaceStrength` and `surfaceScale`, none by default; the sculpted-clay,
  paper-craft and miniature-diorama presets set it.

Fixed:

- heavy-ink-shadows draws its shadows as solid black ink
  (`shadowDarkness 1`), with a near-black sky, a blood-red haze and muted
  colour.
- low-resolution-3d ends its draw distance in a short dark fog the colour
  of its sky.
- `MaterialStyle.surface` no longer draws black blots (a NaN) where the
  shading normal lies in the face; Rumpus Isle's look sets `surface none`.
