# Deleting `examples/`: what the examples checked, and where each check lives now

This is the owner's decision of 2026-11-06, briefed by thread T-38e88112. The base is
origin/main 20afa0802, on branch `delete-examples` (lane-25).

The rule: a template is tried first, because the templates ship to users.
A fixture under `tests/fixtures/<name>/` is built only where no template
exercises the behaviour. A dependent that existed only to exercise an
example is deleted.

## Who read `examples/`

There were three kinds of reader:

1. **Tests and tools that read an example's files.** These are listed in A and B.
2. **Each dogfood game's `tests/frames.og.ts` smoke.** It ran two frames
   and a few `get:Game` facts. No lane runs these smokes; only
   web-reference ran brasswick's and meridian-loop's, at a wave's end.
   These are listed in C.
3. **Comments that cite an example's FINDINGS number** as the provenance of a
   regression test. The tests themselves are self-contained (settle,
   resource-writes, mid-tick save, armed emitters, restored event,
   og-run's end tick, web text entry, and so on). They keep running
   unchanged. Each comment that pointed a reader at a path under
   `examples/` is reworded.

## Notes on what is dropped

- **The art stage's committed references** (decided by the owner,
  2026-11-06). The art-* scenes and their references are deleted; the
  authored art a look relies on moved into the look's directory. The art
  stage is a capture for the art director's review, with no diff.
- **The flat look's lawn regression** (flat.rs): see its row.
- **Game logic of the dogfood games.** The lap timer, the ghost, plates and
  doors, and the minigames were the games' own code, not engine features.
  The engine features under them stay covered as listed.
