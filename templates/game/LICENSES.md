## Provenance note (integrator, 2026-10-17)

`assets/models/trickster.glb` and `assets/models/dropship.glb` are copies of
models from OriginGame's own `origingame-examples` repository, used here as
fixtures. Whether they ship in the public template is the owner's call; the
phase-1 M6 round replaces them with engine-owned or CC0 models.
