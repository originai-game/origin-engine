# midcore

Generated by `og style new` (docs/ui.md §12.5) for the mobile midcore strategy look: its
`style.ogt` and `sfx-*.ogt` are the engine's own work, under the
engine's licence. Its sounds are synthesised by the cook from `type sfx`
presets; no recording is bundled.

It draws with built-in fonts and icons, which stay where they are and
keep their licences (each pack's `LICENSES.md` and `OFL.txt`):

- `@styles/cartoon/icons.png`
- `@styles/cjk/NotoSansSC-Bold.ttf`
- `@styles/cjk/ZCOOLQingKeHuangYou-Regular.ttf`
- `@styles/shared/Rubik-SemiBold.ttf`
- `@styles/shared/TitanOne-Regular.ttf`
