# clean

Started by `og style new` (docs/ui.md §3.3) for the minimal modern look and
authored by hand from there. Its `style.ogt` and `sfx-*.ogt` are the engine's
own work, under the engine's licence. The cook synthesises its sounds from
`type sfx` presets; no recording is bundled. It draws only flat fills,
strokes and effects, so it has no atlas and requests no art.

It draws with built-in fonts and icons. They stay where they are and keep
their own licences (each pack's `LICENSES.md` and `OFL.txt`):

- `@styles/shared/BarlowSemiCondensed-SemiBold.ttf` (display and numeric roles)
- `@styles/shared/InstrumentSans-Regular.ttf` (body)
- `@styles/cjk/NotoSansSC-Regular.ttf`
- `@styles/cjk/NotoSansSC-Bold.ttf`
- `@styles/shared/ink-icons.png`
