# The shared fonts and icons: licences

Fonts and icon sheets the generated seed styles draw with (`og style new`,
docs/ui.md §12.5). They came from the paper and neon packs, which were
folded into storybook and cyber (the owner, 2026-11-02), and from the jade
and midcore packs; this directory is not a style.
